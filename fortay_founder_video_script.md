# Founder video script for the Fortay Connect homepage

90 seconds. Direct to camera, mid shot. Office or studio. No music behind the voice. Light editing only.

The point of this video is to get Harriet on screen as the founder, anchored on a real story (the construction company that bought Zoom phone and rejected it), so the website has a human anchor that the competition does not.

## Production notes

Tone: confident, slightly provocative, intelligent. Not corporate, not over rehearsed. Harriet talks to the camera like she would to a buyer in a room.
Sentence case captions only. Lowercase, no full stops, two lines max per caption.
B roll: Mark Taylor on a call, an engineer at a screen, a real Fortay platform dashboard if available. Use sparingly. Voice carries the piece.
Run time: 85 seconds plus 5 seconds black at end. Total 90.
File name on delivery: `fortay-founder-90s-v1.mp4`. Replace the `HERO_VIDEO_URL` constant in `src/components/home/SoundFamiliarSection.tsx` with the hosted path.

## Script

[0 to 8 seconds, cold open, hard cut from black]

I'm Harriet, one of the two founders of Fortay Connect. Last year a £25 million construction firm in the midlands bought Zoom phone, rolled it out, and four months later told us they'd rejected it.

[8 to 20 seconds, build]

Adoption was 18%. The CFO had signed off £40,000. The Operations Director was about to stand in front of the board and admit it had not worked. The platform was not the problem. The rollout was.

[20 to 35 seconds, the diagnosis]

What the supplier had done was sell licences. What the client needed was change management. Training. Configuration that mapped to how the business actually runs. Nobody had done any of that. So the team just kept using the old system on their mobiles.

[35 to 50 seconds, what Fortay did]

We rebuilt the rollout in 90 days. Same platform. Different deployment. Adoption went from 18 to 92 percent. The CFO got the number she needed for the board. The Operations Director got the project off the risk register.

[50 to 65 seconds, the point]

That is what we keep being called in to do. Not pick the platform. Pick the platform, then make it land. We do not sell licences. We sell the result, and then we measure it for years afterwards.

[65 to 80 seconds, the offer]

If you have bought communications or AI kit and it is not landing, that is the conversation we want. Not the demo. The honest conversation about what went wrong and what to do about it.

[80 to 90 seconds, close]

Thirty minutes with me or Mark, on us. Press the button and pick a slot.

[Cut to black, 5 seconds, end card with logo and "fortayconnect.com"]

## End card text

Fortay Connect

CX, communications and AI transformation that pays back inside one year

fortayconnect.com

## Why these choices

The construction company is the right anchor because it is honest about a failed rollout, not a victory lap. That makes everything else more credible. Most consultancies cannot show a story like this because their case studies are sanitised by the customer's marketing team. Yours can be different.

The "we sell the result, then we measure it for years afterwards" line is the core differentiation. It is the 98% retention claim made in human language.

The "we do not sell licences" line is a deliberate competitor jab without naming. The buyer will know who you mean.

Closing on "thirty minutes with me or Mark" puts the founders on the line. This is what the calls are showing already (Mark named in 14 of the 19 calls) and the website should make it explicit.

## Shooting notes

Shoot in one go, no script reading. Print the script, internalise it, then talk to the camera. Stumble allowed. Three or four takes maximum. Pick the one where the energy is highest, not the one where every word is perfect.

If you want to test it before committing budget, shoot a phone version on a Monday morning, send it to three named buyers in your pipeline, and ask them whether it sounds like you. Then commission the proper version.

## Variants for testing

A 30 second version cuts everything before "what we keep being called in to do" and tightens the offer. Use that for paid social and LinkedIn ads.

A 2 minute version expands the diagnosis section with a second story (one of the Anthony Gold or Covermore patterns, anonymised) for the About page or sales decks.
